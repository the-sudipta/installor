# Roadmap

The roadmap communicates direction, not a delivery guarantee. Work is prioritized through evidence, safety impact, cross-platform value, and maintainer capacity.

## Near term

- Complete the published manual QA matrix on macOS and Linux.
- Add signed and notarized release guidance for institutional deployment.
- Improve validation and previews for imported spreadsheet rows.
- Add structured export and backup controls that clearly exclude credentials.
- Expand accessibility and keyboard-navigation testing.

## Research and engineering directions

- Provenance-aware package metadata and policy evaluation.
- Reproducible machine profiles without collecting sensitive device inventories.
- Differential reconciliation and explainable conflict resolution.
- Verifiable installer manifests, signatures, SBOMs, and transparency data.
- Usability studies comparing explicit confirmation with fully automatic deployment.
- Organization-managed catalogs with role-based policy overlays.

## Not planned as defaults

- Silent background monitoring.
- Bypassing operating-system security controls.
- Uploading local inventory without explicit configuration.
- Automatically trusting detected applications or heuristic file matches.

Propose scoped work through a [feature request](https://github.com/the-sudipta/installor/issues/new?template=feature.yml). Research and funded collaboration proposals are described in [FUNDING.md](FUNDING.md).
