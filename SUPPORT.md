## Useful diagnostic information

For a public bug report, include the Installor version, operating system/version, source type, installer type, expected behavior, actual behavior, and minimal reproduction steps. Redact usernames from paths and remove tokens, Sheet URLs that expose private data, software-license keys, institutional data, and database contents.

Community support is provided on a best-effort basis. A public issue is not a service-level agreement. Commercial support, sponsored features, or research collaboration require a separate written scope.
