# Responsible Use Policy

Installor is designed for software catalogs and installation workflows on systems the user is authorized to manage.

Do not use Installor to:

- deploy malware, unwanted software, credential stealers, surveillance tools, or deceptive installers;
- bypass organizational policy, software licensing, operating-system safeguards, or required administrator approval;
- collect or publish another person's software inventory without appropriate notice and authorization;
- expose private Google Sheets, shared secrets, institutional records, license keys, or personal data;
- automate academic misconduct, misrepresent research evidence, or fabricate test results;
- target protected groups, facilitate discrimination, or cause unlawful harm.

Users are responsible for the legality, licensing, provenance, signatures, checksums, and suitability of every catalog entry and installer. Organizations should evaluate Installor under their own security, privacy, procurement, and change-management controls before deployment.
