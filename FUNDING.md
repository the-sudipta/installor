# Funding and Collaboration

Installor is seeking evidence-driven collaborations that strengthen dependable, transparent, and user-controlled software deployment.

## Suitable engagement models

<table width="100%">
  <tr><th width="28%" align="left">Model</th><th width="72%" align="left">Possible scope</th></tr>
  <tr><td>Research collaboration</td><td>Joint study design, reproducible experiments, datasets composed of synthetic or appropriately governed metadata, and peer-reviewed dissemination.</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Industrial PhD</td><td>A multi-year programme around software provenance, policy-aware automation, human factors, or cross-platform reconciliation with academic and industry supervision.</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Sponsored engineering</td><td>Auditable platform support, enterprise policy layers, accessibility, signing infrastructure, or carefully scoped integrations.</td></tr>
  <tr><td>Pilot evaluation</td><td>A bounded deployment in a laboratory or organization with agreed success measures, data governance, risk ownership, and feedback.</td></tr>
</table>

## What support would fund

- Research design, ethics/governance preparation, controlled experiments, and publication.
- Cross-platform hardware, virtualized test infrastructure, signing/notarization, and accessibility evaluation.
- Security analysis, software-supply-chain metadata, reproducible fixtures, and long-term maintenance.
- Student or research-assistant time where governed through an eligible institution.

## Start a conversation

Contact [`the-sudipta`](https://github.com/the-sudipta) through the verified GitHub profile and reference **Installor collaboration**. A useful first message identifies the organization or research group, problem, intended users, target platforms, approximate timeline, funding model, and any data or compliance constraints.

No sponsors, grants, affiliations, or institutional endorsements are claimed at this time. Funding and commercial work begin only after a separate written agreement covering scope, ownership, licensing, confidentiality, data protection, publication, and acceptance criteria.
